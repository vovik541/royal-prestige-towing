Questions people ask

Is RPT only for luxury cars?

No. Exotics are a big part of what we do, but most loads are everyday vehicles, vans and equipment. Every one gets the same care.

Do you use an open or enclosed trailer?

The loads on this page traveled on our open flatbed car-hauler. If your vehicle needs enclosed transport, mention it when you ask for a quote and we’ll talk through options.

Can you move machinery that has to be lifted on?

Yes, when there’s a forklift or loading equipment on site — like the lathe in our photos. Send dimensions, weight and a photo, and we’ll tell you if it fits.

Can you take a vehicle that doesn’t run?

Tell us whether it rolls, steers and brakes. We’ll confirm before pickup whether we can load it safely.

How is the price worked out?

By distance, the kind of load and how easy pickup and drop-off are. Ask for a quote and you’ll get one clear number.

Do you go to the Bay Area?

We’re based in Sacramento and are confirming our regular Bay Area coverage. Call with your route and we’ll tell you straight away.

What’s going on the deck?

Tell us what it is and where it needs to be. You’ll get a straight answer and one clear price.
