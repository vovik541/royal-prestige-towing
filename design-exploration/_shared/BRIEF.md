# Royal Prestige Towing — Homepage Design Exploration (original brief, condensed faithfully)

Role: senior creative director / award-level web designer / UX strategist / brand identity specialist.

## Task
Three completely different, premium homepage design concepts as browsable, responsive, interactive prototypes.
DESIGN EXPLORATION ONLY: no Angular, no SSR, no backend, no CMS, no translation system, no deployment, no commits.
The future site will be Angular SSR in English (primary), Ukrainian and Russian — not part of this task, but designs should extend to it (e.g. a language switch placeholder, copy that tolerates ~30% longer strings).

## Business
- Royal Prestige Towing (RPT). Owner: Serhii Hoida. Primary location: Sacramento, California.
- Potential territory: Northern California incl. Sacramento and the San Francisco Bay Area — anything beyond Sacramento must be presented as PROVISIONAL / "call to confirm".
- Transports: luxury & exotic vehicles (McLaren, Lamborghini), regular passenger cars, SUVs and commercial vehicles, industrial machinery & equipment, specialty loads, longer distances.
- NOT exclusively luxury. Luxury is the visual differentiator; ordinary customers must feel welcome. Professional, trustworthy, premium transport for valuable cargo of all kinds.
- NOT a generic emergency roadside towing site.
- NEVER invent: 24/7 availability, licensing, insurance coverage, years of experience, reviews/testimonials, certifications, fleet size, guarantees, statistics.

## Assets
Use the REAL supplied photos (see ASSETS.md). Crop/frame/darken/compose allowed; preserve authenticity. No stock photography replacing the portfolio. Design intelligently around the photos' limits (low resolution ~939px wide, phone snapshots).

## Three directions must differ substantially in
Art direction, palette, typography, hero composition, layout system, navigation, section transitions, photo use, hierarchy, interaction/animation, storytelling. Each like a different high-end studio. All primarily DARK, interpreted differently. Not default black+gold. No cliché crown icons. Not a limousine template.

## Homepage content (may be reorganized/combined per concept)
1 Header & navigation · 2 High-impact hero + primary CTA · 3 Trust/positioning statement · 4 Transportation services · 5 Luxury/exotic spotlight · 6 Featured real projects · 7 Why choose RPT · 8 Meet the owner — Serhii · 9 Service territory (provisional coverage clearly marked) · 10 How transportation works · 11 FAQ · 12 Strong final contact/quote CTA · 13 Footer.
Must quickly communicate: what is transported, where, why trust, how to get a quote or call. Natural American English, realistic business copy.

## Brand identity per concept
Brand mood · color system with HEX · typography pairing · logo / RPT wordmark · visual language · photographic style · button & nav styling · ONE signature interactive feature that improves UX (not decoration).

## Implementation
Separate, independently viewable prototypes in design-exploration/concept-0X/. Real responsive layouts. Tasteful, performant transitions; respect prefers-reduced-motion. Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

## Responsive
Must work at 1440, 768 and 390px. Mobile is especially important: intentional compositions, readable type, usable navigation, prominent CTAs, good crops — not a shrunken desktop.

## Avoid / prefer
Avoid: generic SaaS layouts, repetitive card grids, template heroes, overused gradients, excessive glassmorphism, arbitrary animation, fake stats/testimonials, stock photos, excessive gold, crowded nav, weak mobile, look-alike concepts.
Prefer: confident type, excellent photo composition, strong hierarchy, whitespace, sophisticated contrast, rhythm, original interaction, distinct personalities, clear conversion paths. Premium without alienating ordinary vehicle owners.

## Validation (per concept)
Render at 1440 / 768 / 390, inspect screenshots, check overflow, broken images, bad crops, unreadable text, inconsistencies; fix; capture final screenshots. Test interactive states (mobile nav, service interactions, CTAs). Never claim visual verification you did not actually look at.
