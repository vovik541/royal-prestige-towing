## Category clichés
1. "#1 / Best / Trusted [City]" SEO headlines. 2. Walls of SEO copy. 3. Utility bar + crowded nav + phone repeated 3–6×. 4. Hero quote widget "Ship From/To — OR — call". 5. Boilerplate trust ("licensed, bonded, insured", "24/7", "100% satisfaction"). 6. Icon-card grids + 3–4 step strips. 7. Stat counters / big review numbers. 8. Templated or stock imagery. 9. Borrowed-prestige logo strips. 10. Sloppy details (contradictory numbers, duplicate testimonials).

## Opportunities for RPT
1. Own the "local premium" gap: local sites are cheap templates; premium sites are national enclosed-only.
2. Real owned photography as the brand — the range (McLaren beside a milling machine) at editorial scale.
3. Present "what we carry" as a range, not a card grid. Nobody pairs supercars with machinery.
4. Show the method (wheel straps, slow loading, wrapped and blocked machinery) instead of claiming it.
5. Make trust verifiable and short (real numbers only when the owner provides them — none yet).
6. Calm conversion path: one primary action on mobile, sticky call/quote, short "what are you moving" request. No popups.
7. Restrained dark palette, editorial typography, few sections, no carousels-for-the-sake-of-it.
8. Coverage shown simply: one clear map or statement naming Sacramento, Bay Area, NorCal corridors (provisional).
