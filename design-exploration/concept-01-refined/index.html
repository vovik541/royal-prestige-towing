Questions people ask

Is RPT only for luxury cars?

No. Exotics are a big part of what we do, but everyday vehicles, vans and equipment are just as welcome — and every one gets the same care.

Do you use an open or enclosed trailer?

The loads on this page traveled on our open flatbed car-hauler. If your vehicle needs enclosed transport, mention it in your request and we’ll talk through options.

Can you move machinery that has to be lifted on?

Yes, when there’s a forklift or loading equipment on site — like the lathe in our photos. Send dimensions, weight and a photo, and we’ll tell you whether it fits.

Can you take a vehicle that doesn’t run?

Tell us whether it rolls, steers and brakes. We’ll confirm before pickup whether we can load it safely.

How is the price worked out?

By distance, the type of load and how easy pickup and drop-off are. Send a request and you’ll get one clear number.

Do you go to the Bay Area?

We’re based in Sacramento and still confirming regular Bay Area coverage. Share your route and we’ll answer straight away.
