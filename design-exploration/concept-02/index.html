Questions, answered plainly

Is RPT only for luxury cars?

No. Exotics are what people notice, but everyday cars, vans and equipment are just as welcome — and get the same care.

Open or enclosed trailer?

Every load on this page rode on our open car-hauler. If you need enclosed transport, say so and we’ll talk through options.

Can you move heavy machinery?

When there’s a forklift or loading equipment at both ends, yes. Send dimensions, weight and a photo and we’ll confirm the fit.

What if it doesn’t run?

Tell us whether it rolls, steers and brakes. We’ll confirm before pickup whether we can load it safely.

How is the price set?

Distance, the kind of load, and access at both ends. You get one clear number before anything is booked.

Do you serve the Bay Area?

We’re based in Sacramento and still confirming regular Bay Area coverage. Call with your route and we’ll answer straight away.

Tell us what needs to move.

Call or send a request. Serhii will get back to you personally.
